# Research-material disclosure

Exact attack instructions, templates, generation implementations, generated fake
reviews and modified product texts are not distributed. Public attack modules
provide category metadata, interfaces, budget validation and external loaders.
They contain no payloads or generation logic.

Qualified researchers may request the restricted reproduction material from
Muhammad Muneer Umar at muneer.umar@kust.edu.pk. Availability remains subject to
upstream rights and an agreed research-use arrangement. CORE and GEO-Bench data
are linked rather than redistributed because no repository license was stated
in the recorded source audit. Their paper licenses do not license their data.

Public metrics contain aggregate statistics and source hashes. No product-level
generated text, raw customer reviews, personal identifiers or model weights are
included. Recreating new attack evaluations requires the restricted material;
regenerating the distributed tables and figures does not.
